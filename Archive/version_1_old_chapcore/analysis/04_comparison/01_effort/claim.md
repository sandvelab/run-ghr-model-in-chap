# Claim

How much did each route cost to find out about and to carry out, measured from its discovery log?

## Children

kind: alternatives
main-path: b_normalised

## Environment

inherits: the project main environment (`environment/`)

## Answers

Route B cost less on **every statistic that orders**, and the answer does not depend on which
of the two readings is taken. The main path is `b_normalised`, because the question is a
comparison and the normalisation is what makes the two logs comparable; `a_asLogged` is kept
runnable because it is the only purely observational reading and is what the normalisation's
effect is visible against.

The difference between the readings is small and in the expected direction: normalising moves
route A from 10 information sources to 9 and route B from 4 to 5, closing part of the gap
without reversing it. Whether the conclusion survives more than this one judgment call is the
stability node's question, not this one's.
